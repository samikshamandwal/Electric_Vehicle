Overview & Mission

Insights Behind the EV Revolution

The electric vehicle market is transforming the automotive industry. Understanding sales performance, consumer preferences, manufacturer competition, and battery technology is essential to understanding this transition. EV Insights brings these insights together through interactive dashboards and data-driven storytelling.

Market Growth

Explore electric vehicle adoption rates, multi-year sales velocity, and volume surges across global automotive territories.

Technology Shift

Understand battery chemistry, energy density breakthroughs, range enhancements, and powertrain architectural shifts.

Consumer Confidence

Explore customer satisfaction indices, review ratings, charging reliability metrics, and category-level vehicle sentiment.

Actionable Insights

Discover strategic patterns, segment opportunities, and competitive benchmarks that empower data-driven decisions.

Full-Stack Integration

About This Project

EV Insights demonstrates the seamless integration of Tableau Public interactive dashboards and narrative stories into a Python Flask web application.

By combining Tableau's enterprise-grade visualization engine with a responsive, dark automotive UI built with clean HTML5, modern CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript, the platform delivers high-end market intelligence without third-party frontend framework overhead.
